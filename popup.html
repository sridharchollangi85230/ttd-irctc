Instructions:

  1. Add passenger details and click "Save Profile"
  2. Go to the IRCTC passenger booking page
  3. Click "Add & Fill All Passengers" in this extension
  4. The extension will open dialogs and auto-fill forms one at a time
  5. Review every passenger before proceeding with payment

⚠️ This extension does NOT handle CAPTCHA, payment, or final booking. You must complete these steps manually.
